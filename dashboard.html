Campus Bulletins & Landmarks

Use the left and right controls to browse through campus landmarks and updates.

Capitol University Gate
The primary gateway and ceremonial portal of Capitol University. All enrolled students and faculty must scan their verified identification upon entering to synchronize security and attendance checkpoints.
Campus Courts
Multi-purpose outdoor athletic courts dedicated to physical education lectures, collegiate tournaments, and student athletic team practices under official faculty oversight.
Friendship Park
The green social heart of the campus. A shaded outdoor communal sanctuary where students assemble between lectures for peer study, collaborative discussions, and university community events.

Campus Academic & Technology Facilities

Key learning spaces, computing environments, and student centers across the campus.

Computer Laboratory A: State-of-the-art computing facility configured with high-performance desktop terminals, network infrastructure, and specialized software development environments for hands-on technical coursework.
Campus Academic Buildings: Expansive multi-story academic complex housing smart lecture halls, collegiate dean offices, and faculty consultation rooms built to support diverse degree programs.
Cyber Park: An open-air campus haven furnished with stone picnic kiosks and surrounding greenery, offering an ergonomic outdoor setting equipped for research, reading, and team consultations.
Students Working Inside CL-A: Active computer laboratory session where students collaborate closely on code debugging, systems architecture projects, and real-time laboratory milestone assessments.
